Delete your account

Last updated: 19 October 2026

1. Deleting in the app (recommended)

If you have the app installed and can access your account, you can delete it yourself in a few steps:

  1. Open Seenit and sign in to your account.
  2. Open the menu (the ☰ icon in the top-left corner) and choose Settings.
  3. Choose Account, scroll down to the Delete account section and tap Delete account permanently.
  4. In the confirmation field, type exactly DELETE (in the Polish version of the app: USUŃ), enter your account password and confirm. If you sign in with Google, the app will ask you to choose your Google account again instead.

Your account and all data associated with it (library, statuses, ratings, watch progress) are deleted immediately and permanently — this cannot be undone.

2. Deleting without access to the app

If you no longer have access to the app (e.g. you uninstalled it, changed devices or forgot your password), email us from the address associated with your account: support@vonmi.app.

In your message, include the email address your account is registered with and say that you want it deleted. We will process your request within 30 days at the latest and confirm the deletion by email.

3. What data is deleted

  • Your account (email address, sign-in data — including the link to your Google account, if you used it).
  • Your entire library: saved movies/TV shows, statuses, ratings and watch progress (watched seasons/episodes).
  • Messages sent through the “Feedback” form.
  • Account data used for aggregate statistics (creation date, day of last use, platform, app language and version) and the username assigned to your account. The username itself stays reserved for 14 days so that nobody can immediately impersonate you — see the Privacy Policy for details.

A full description of the data we process and on what basis can be found in our Privacy Policy.
