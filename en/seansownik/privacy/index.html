Seenit

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

1. Data controller

The controller of personal data collected in the Seenit app (Polish name: Seansownik) is Sebastian Lech, contact: support@vonmi.app.

2. What data we collect

Seenit requires an account so that it can save your movie and TV show library and sync it across your devices. For this purpose we process:

  • Account data — when you sign up with email: your email address and password (we never receive your password in plain text — it is handled directly by Google’s Firebase Authentication). When you sign in with Google: the email address, name and profile picture shared by your Google account. When you sign in with Apple (iPhone, iPad): the email address shared by Apple — if you choose “Hide My Email”, this is a random relay address from Apple, not your real one.
  • Username — every account has a unique username. By default we create it from the part of your email address before the “@” sign (never the whole address) and you can change it in Settings, at most once every 3 days. We also store the date of your last username change. Currently only you can see your username; if features are added in the future where it becomes visible to others, we will tell you in advance.
  • Library data — the movies/TV shows you add to your library, the status you assign (Planned/Watching/Up to date/Completed/Dropped), ratings, your watch progress (watched seasons/episodes together with the dates you watched them, which we use for your statistics, such as your year in review) and whether you muted notifications for a title.
  • Basic account usage data — with your account we store the date it was created, the day you last used the app (the date only, without the time), the platform (Android, iOS or browser), the app language and the app version. We also store the days on which you used the app (dates only, without the time) and, in the Android and iOS apps, a list of devices used with your account: a random app installation identifier (not a phone identifier or an advertising identifier; reinstalling the app creates a new one), the platform, the app version, the date the device was added and the day it was last used. A device entry is deleted when you sign out on that device. This data is used solely for aggregate statistics, such as the number of active accounts per day and the number of devices with the app. We do not record which screens you open or what you tap.
  • Feedback — if you send a message through the “Feedback” form, we store its type (bug, idea, other), content, date and status, together with the app version, platform, language and the app screen you chose the message is about. The message is linked to your account so you can see its status. We keep it until you delete your account.
  • Crash reports (Android and iOS app only) — when an error occurs in the app, a technical report is sent: where in the code the error happened, device model, OS and app version, memory state and a random installation identifier. The report does not include your email address, account data or library. Reports are handled by Firebase Crashlytics and kept for about 90 days. You can turn them off at any time in the app’s Settings (“Send crash reports”).
  • Technical data stored only locally on your device — a cache of search results and title details (so the app runs faster and works offline) and app settings (e.g. theme, language, notification settings). We do not collect this data or send it to any servers outside your device. Premiere notifications, if you turn them on, are scheduled locally on your device based on your library; no external notification service is involved.

The app currently contains no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking tools, and does not collect behavioral data for marketing purposes. Crash reports are used solely to detect and fix bugs. From the data in the database we keep only our own aggregate statistics: the number of accounts, the number of active accounts, the split by platform and language, and the number of library entries by status.

3. Purpose and legal basis of processing

We process data to provide the service — to create and maintain your account and to save and sync your library across devices. The legal basis is that processing is necessary to perform the contract for providing an electronic service (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and, when you sign in with Google or Apple, also your consent given by choosing that sign-in method (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Crash reports are processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the app stable (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); you can object by turning crash reports off in Settings. On the same basis we process basic account usage data and keep aggregate statistics — our legitimate interest is knowing how many people use the app and on which platforms.

4. Who we share data with

To run the app we use external service providers that process data on our behalf or at our request:

  • Google Firebase (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC) — authentication (Firebase Authentication), storage of account and library data (Cloud Firestore, region europe-central2, Warsaw), crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) and checking that connections to the database come from the genuine app (Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity on Android, Apple’s App Attest and DeviceCheck on iPhone and iPad, and reCAPTCHA in the browser) — to protect against abuse.
  • The Movie Database (TMDb) — the source of movie/TV show metadata (titles, descriptions, posters, release dates). Search queries sent to TMDb are not linked to your account or any other personal data — they are ordinary requests for publicly available information about titles.

We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Transfers outside the European Economic Area

Google’s infrastructure (Firebase) may in some cases process data on servers outside the EEA — this is done on the basis of compliance mechanisms recognized by the GDPR, including the standard contractual clauses used by Google.

6. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account together with all related data at any time, on your own, in the app (Settings → Delete account → Delete account permanently) — deletion is permanent and immediate. Exception: after you change your username or delete your account, the previous username stays reserved for 14 days so that nobody can immediately impersonate you. The reservation record contains only the username itself, a technical account identifier and the release date; it remains until someone else takes the name. After account deletion two technical records without personal data other than the account identifier also remain: the date of the last username change and the counter of feedback messages sent. See also how to delete your account.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, rectify it, erase it, restrict its processing, port it and object to its processing. You can exercise most of these rights yourself in the app (editing/deleting library entries, deleting your account). For anything else, write to the address given in the “Contact” section. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Poland, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

8. Data security

Access to library data in the Firestore database is restricted by security rules to the account owner only — no other user of the app can access it. Passwords of accounts created with email are handled entirely by Firebase Authentication and are never stored by us in plain text.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

10. Changes to this privacy policy

This policy may be updated as the app evolves. The date of the last update is always shown at the top of this page.
