Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what data the Spendio app processes, why, and what control you have over it. In short: your entries belong to you, they are stored in the European Union, nothing is sold or used for advertising, and you can delete everything at any time.

1. Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is Sebastian Lech, the developer of Spendio (publishing as “vonmi”). Contact: support@vonmi.app.

2. Data we process

Account data

Spendio requires an account so your data can sync between your devices. You sign in with Apple (on iPhone and iPad) or with Google. We receive from the sign-in provider a unique user ID, your email address (with Apple this can be a private relay address if you choose “Hide My Email”) and, if you allow it, your name. We never see your Apple or Google password.

What you enter in the app

Expenses and income (amount, currency, category, date, note, tags), your categories, budgets, recurring entries and app settings such as your main currency. This is the data the app exists for. It is stored in your account and cached on your devices so the app also works offline.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, it sends a crash report: technical details of the error, device model, operating system version, app version and a random installation identifier. Crash reports do not contain your entries. Crash reporting is on by default and you can turn it off in the app settings.

Usage statistics (only with your consent)

If you agree, the app sends anonymous usage statistics, for example which screens are used, together with device type, operating system, country and a random app instance identifier. This helps us decide what to improve. Statistics are off until you agree, and you can withdraw consent in the settings at any time. Advertising identifiers are never collected and advertising features are disabled.

What we do not do

  • No ads, no selling or sharing of your data for marketing.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No access to your bank accounts.
  • Reminders are scheduled on your device; no push notification server is involved.

4. Where data is stored and who processes it

Spendio uses Google Firebase (Google Ireland Limited) as its processor: Firebase Authentication for sign-in, Cloud Firestore for your data, Firebase Crashlytics for crash reports and Google Analytics for Firebase for usage statistics. Your entries are stored in Google’s European multi-region data centres (Belgium and the Netherlands). Google may process some technical data, such as crash reports and statistics, outside the EU; such transfers are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

Apple and Google act as independent sign-in providers under their own privacy policies.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account data and your entries: until you delete your account. Deletion removes them from our database immediately.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Usage statistics: up to 2 months.
  • Emails to support: as long as needed to handle your request, at most 2 years.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to correct it, to have it deleted, to restrict processing, to data portability, to object to processing based on legitimate interest, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. To use these rights, write to support@vonmi.app. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, in Poland the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO), or the authority in your country.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all your data in the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. You can also request deletion by email. Details: Delete your account.

8. Children

Spendio is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections. Database access rules ensure that only you, when signed in, can read and change your data.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will inform you in the app.
