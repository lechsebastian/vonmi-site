Delete your account

Last updated: 24 November 2026

You can delete your Spendio account and all data connected to it at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Spendio and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. If you signed in with Apple, you may be asked to confirm with Apple once more.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out on this device. Other devices are signed out the next time they connect.

Option 2: by email

If you no longer have the app, send an email to support@vonmi.app from the email address of your account (for Apple “Hide My Email”, from the address it forwards to) with the subject Delete my Spendio account. We will delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner, and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in data.
  • All expenses, income, categories, budgets, recurring entries and settings.
  • The link between Spendio and your Apple sign-in is revoked.

What is kept and for how long

  • Crash reports, which do not contain your entries: deleted automatically after up to 90 days.
  • Usage statistics, if you consented: deleted automatically after up to 2 months.
  • Your emails with support: up to 2 years, then deleted.

Uninstalling the app does not delete your account. Active subscriptions bought in the App Store or Google Play must be cancelled in your store account.
